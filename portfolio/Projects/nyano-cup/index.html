Our Journey

The Spirit of Nyano Cup

"Nyano Cup" (न्यानो कप) means a truly warm and comforting cup. Our coffee path wanders through mist-covered mountain hillsides in Gulmi, Nuwakot, and Lalitpur.

Grown naturally in mineral-rich soil under protective mountain shade, these cherries carry distinctive profiles of dark cocoa, mild citrus accents, and deep caramelized sweetness.
